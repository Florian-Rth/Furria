# An intake invites on its starter's decision

Florian, 2026-11-19: a board member — or a trainer, for her own group — starts a *Neuzugang*,
the newcomer fills in her details from a QR code, and if she gave an email the app invitation
goes out by itself. Two earlier rulings stood against that: the club's invitations are **never
sent by the system on its own**, and a **group admin issues none** (2026-09-25).

## The decision

**Starting an intake is the decision to invite.** When the intake completes and she gave an email
and is eligible (the club's configured age of consent — nothing hard-coded), the invitation is
issued with the intake's **starter as issuer**, by mail, and her closing screen may redeem it on
the spot. This holds for an intake a **group admin** started too: the only keys such an account
gets come from the group membership the trainer just gave her, so the harm the 2026-09-25 rule
guarded against — a group handing out club access — does not arise. Every other path stays as it
was: a group admin still cannot invite an existing person, and nothing else invites unasked.

## Considered options

- **No invitation from a group admin's intake** — she would land as *kann eingeladen werden*
  and wait for `persons.manage`. Rejected: the newcomer is standing there with her phone; the
  point of the intake is that nobody has to follow up.
- **Never invite from an intake; invite by hand afterwards** — rejected for the same reason.
