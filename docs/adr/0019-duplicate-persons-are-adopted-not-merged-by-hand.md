# Duplicate persons are adopted, not merged by hand

Public self-registration for ticket buyers (decided 2026-08-18) creates persons with no
affiliation, so the same human can exist twice: once as a ticket buyer, once in the club's
register. Decided with Florian on 2026-09-25 while shaping accounts and invitations, closing the
open question *guest registration & duplicates* in `CONTEXT.md`.

## The decision

**Nobody in the club hunts for duplicates.** Two rules close them.

1. **Prevention by adoption.** When a manager records a person whose email belongs to an existing
   person with no affiliation, the editor offers to adopt that person instead of creating a second
   one.
2. **Claim-in on redemption.** If a duplicate slips through, she closes it herself: redeeming an
   invitation to an address that already has an account asks her to sign in with that account.
   Signing in proves she owns it; the account then moves onto the club's person, and the club's
   person absorbs the stray one together with everything it holds (orders, tickets).

## Considered options

- **An admin merge tool.** Rejected: it makes duplicates the club's work, and a manager cannot
  prove that two records are the same human — only she can, by signing in.
- **Claim-in without prevention.** Rejected: adoption at creation costs one suggestion in an editor
  and avoids the merge entirely in the common case.

## Consequences

- An account's link to its person can change. Nothing may treat an account's person as fixed for
  the account's lifetime.
- Absorbing a person is irreversible, so it happens only on her sign-in, never on a manager's
  judgement.
