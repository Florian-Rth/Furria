04 · Working music

Musik schneiden

While the dance is being made: several files, put in order, each cut to a start and an end point.

Musik3 Dateien · zusammen 0:30,0
Zählzeiten128 BPM erkannt · erste 1 bei 0:00,2
  • Drag the handles on a waveform to cut. When one file reaches its end point, playback jumps straight to the next file's start point.
  • Steps and movements stay tied to their moment in the song. Re-cutting moves them along; anything in a cut-away range gets flagged.
  • The worker finds the beat, so the editor can snap to counts 1–8. The first "1" and the tempo can be corrected by hand.

06 · Formation and movement

Bühne bearbeiten

Place spots, drag them, and set from which count to which count they move.

Schritt 4 · Kreis bildenTeil A · Zählzeit 1–8
6 ausgewählt
Bewegung der AuswahlZählzeit 1 – 4
  • Spots, not names: the choreography uses Platz 1–12, and people are assigned to spots. A stand-in only swaps the person.
  • Drag a dot to place it. A tap adds or removes a dot from the selection. Grab a curve's middle to bend it.
  • Pull the bar to set when the selection moves: drag either end, and it snaps to the counts. Preview plays it with the music.
  • The stage background is our real stage, drawn in Inkscape: size, floor marks 1–9 and the stairs, as on the real one.
